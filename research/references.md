# References Organized by Chapter

## Ch 1: Bayes

1. **Bayes, T. and Price, R. (1763).** "An Essay Towards Solving a Problem in the Doctrine of Chances." *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London*, 53, 370-418. Foundational. Posthumous publication via Price.
2. **Cox, R. T. (1946).** "Probability, Frequency and Reasonable Expectation." *American Journal of Physics*, 14, 1-13. The original Cox argument.
3. **Cox, R. T. (1961).** *The Algebra of Probable Inference.* Johns Hopkins. Book-length elaboration.
4. **Jaynes, E. T. (2003).** *Probability Theory: The Logic of Science.* Cambridge. The Cox-Jaynes consolidation; Chapter 2 is the standard pedagogical statement.
5. **Van Horn, K. S. (2003).** "Constructing a Logic of Plausible Inference: A Guide to Cox's Theorem." *International Journal of Approximate Reasoning*. The careful modern treatment.
6. **Halpern, J. Y. (1999).** "A Counterexample to Theorems of Cox and Fine." *J. AI Research.* ([arXiv:1105.5450](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.5450)). The famous finite-domain counterexample.
7. **Terenin, A. and Draper, D. (2017).** "Cox's Theorem and the Jaynesian Interpretation of Probability." ([arXiv:1507.06597](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.06597)). Recent extension addressing Halpern's objections.
8. **Hacking, I. (2006).** *The Emergence of Probability* (2nd ed.). Cambridge. Historical context.
